How FlowMed Nexus Works

From Fragmented Logs to Coordinated Action

1

Unified Operational Ingestion

Aggregates triage arrivals, ward bed statuses, lab specimen stages, and partner hospital capacity into a single live state.

2

Predictive & RAG Intelligence

Compares real-time demand against estimated arrival curves and retrieves relevant hospital SOPs for surge or delay protocols.

3

Explainable Agent Recommendations

Specialized agents surface bottlenecks with transparent reasoning chains—never making clinical diagnoses.

4

Human-Approved Execution & Audit

Administrators and coordinators review and approve proposed reallocations or inter-hospital requests with full audit logging.
